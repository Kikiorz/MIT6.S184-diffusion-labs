## 仓库概述 (Repository Overview)

This repository contains educational Jupyter notebooks for **MIT 6.S184/6.S975: Generative AI with Stochastic Differential Equations**, taught at MIT during IAP 2025. The repository includes:

- **labs/**: Student-facing lab notebooks covering diffusion models and stochastic differential equations
- **solutions/**: Complete solution notebooks for instructors and reference
- Educational materials on Langevin dynamics, score parameterizations, and conditional sampling
